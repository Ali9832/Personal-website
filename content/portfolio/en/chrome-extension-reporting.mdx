## The brief

The audience ranges from hospital managers and industrial safety leads to researchers and course participants. The site therefore had to support service discovery, product browsing, training registration and technical contact.

## Information architecture

The structure is organised around training, design and development, equipment, specialist services, and projects and solutions. Separate paths cover dosimetry, radiation protection, calibration and equipment maintenance, alongside the shop and academy.

## Outcome

The website moved beyond a simple company profile to become a multi-purpose platform for capability, equipment sales and specialist education.
