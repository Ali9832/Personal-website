## The brief

Curtain buyers usually ask three things: which models exist, what it will cost for their window, and who measures and installs. The home page had to answer all three and lead to a consultation or order request.

## What was delivered

- A home page with two primary actions: online price estimate and catalogue.
- Four categories — zebra, roller (shade), eyelet and classic — each with its own carousel.
- A price form taking window width, height and curtain type, next to on-site measuring, installation and transparent pricing.
- Trust sections: on-site measuring, fast delivery, quality guarantee, support, past work and reviews.
- An accessible mobile menu and responsive RTL carousels.

## Outcome

A complete front end for a curtain store home page, covering product discovery through to a consultation request, ready to be wired into WordPress or WooCommerce.

> This is a front-end concept project, not a live client site. Contact details and reviews in the design are placeholders, and the price form is not yet connected to calculation logic.
