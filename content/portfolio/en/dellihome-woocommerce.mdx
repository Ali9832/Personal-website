## The brief

Decor products are sold through imagery, colour and atmosphere as much as specifications. The storefront needed to put product photography first while keeping the mobile buying path short.

## What was delivered

- A warm, minimal custom WordPress theme for home decor.
- WooCommerce integration for products, pricing, cart and checkout.
- AJAX live search for faster product discovery.
- Responsive sliders for products and inspiration imagery.
- Custom product page, gallery, tabs and related-products layout.
- A payment-ready checkout flow prepared for Zarinpal.

## Outcome

DelliHome became a visual, brand-led shopping experience combining a custom theme, WooCommerce commerce flows and live search in a Persian responsive interface.
