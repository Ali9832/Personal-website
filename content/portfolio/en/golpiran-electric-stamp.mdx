## The brief

Golpiran needed a site that could introduce electric stamping machines to offices, organisations and stamp-making partners, guiding visitors from product discovery to quote requests and bulk cooperation.

## What was delivered

- A homepage with clear product value, benefits and quote CTA.
- Two primary product paths: electric embossing and electric gelatin stamping.
- A dedicated wholesale and partner section.
- An enquiry form with device type and order type selection.
- Shop, product, cart, about and contact pages.
- A WordPress and WooCommerce-ready theme structure for real products and payment integration.

## Outcome

The project became more than a product brochure: product education, retail purchase, wholesale partnership and consultation are connected in one coherent experience.
