## The brief

Parsafza sells ready-made products and accepts custom print orders. The goal was to turn an unclear, conversation-led process into a guided experience where visitors choose filament amount, material and colour before requesting a final quote.

## What was delivered

- A homepage centred on the message “Build your idea at a real price”.
- A calculator for PLA, ABS and PETG with selectable colours.
- Clear sections for useful parts, tools, organisers, custom products and print services.
- Shop, search, cart, custom-file submission and nationwide delivery messaging.

## Outcome

Parsafza now works as both a product catalogue and an entry point for custom manufacturing, with a clearer estimate before final file review.

> The calculator displays an initial estimate; the final price is confirmed after the 3D file is reviewed.
