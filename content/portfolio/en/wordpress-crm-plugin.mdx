## The brief

The home page had to do more than introduce the doctor. Patients needed to understand the difference between general orthopaedics, infant care, paediatric services and adult treatment, then reach an appointment path quickly.

## What was delivered

- Calm, professional visual language for a personal medical brand.
- Separate content paths for adults, infants, newborns and general orthopaedics.
- Doctor profile, education, treatment approach, FAQ and health magazine sections.
- Appointment request, direct call, address and reception hours in accessible locations.

## Outcome

The website became a clear point of contact for expertise, patient education and appointment requests across mobile and desktop.

> The figures describe the delivered scope, not a claim of appointment growth.
